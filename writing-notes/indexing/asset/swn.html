Watts-Strogatz Small-World Simulator

Parameters

Nodes (N) 15

Total number of nodes in the network.

Nearest Neighbors (K) 4

Initial connections per node (must be even). High K means dense local clusters.

Rewiring Prob. (p) 0.05

Chance to redirect a local edge to a random node. 0 = Lattice, 1 = Random Graph.
